Duas for rain and drought (Sahih al-Bukhari 986)

Hisn al-Muslim 63

((اللَّهُمَّ أَغِثْنَا، اللَّهُمَّ أَغِثْنَا، اللَّهُمَّ أَغِثْنَا)).

(Allahumma aghithna, allahumma aghithna, allahumma aghithna.)

(O Allah, relieve us, O Allah, relieve us, O Allah, relieve us.)

Hisn al-Muslim 63 · Hisn al-Muslim

Source & authenticity

Context & meaning

The same three words, repeated three times, with the address restated before each. Nothing is added between the repetitions and nothing is named as the thing to be relieved from. The whole supplication consists of one request and its repetition, and it is plural throughout — relieve us, never relieve me.

When & how to recite

Bukhari records this from Sharik, among the supplications for rain.

Reflection

The chapter places it with requests for rain, yet rain is never mentioned. What is asked for is relief, and what the relief is from is left entirely unstated.
