Dua for qunut in the witr… (Riyad as-Salihin 751 · 32:117)

Hisn al-Muslim 32

((اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِرِضَاكَ مِنْ سَخَطِكَ، وَبِمُعَافَاتِكَ مِنْ عُقُوبَتِكَ، وَأَعُــــوذُ بِكَ مِنْكَ، لاَ أُحْصِي ثَنَاءً عَلَيْكَ، أَنْتَ كَمَا أَثْنَيْتَ عَلَى نَفْسِكَ)).

(Allahumma innee aAAoothu biridaka min sakhatik, wabimuAAafatika min AAuqoobatik, wa-aAAoothu bika mink, la ohsee thana-an AAalayk, anta kama athnayta AAala nafsik.)

(O Allah, I take refuge within Your pleasure from Your displeasure and within Your pardon from Your punishment, and I take refuge in You from You. I cannot enumerate Your praise. You are as You have praised Yourself.)

Context & meaning

Refuge is sought three times and the construction tightens with each. The first sets two of His attributes against each other, pleasure against displeasure. The second does the same with pardon and punishment. The third strips the attributes away and leaves only the pronoun standing on both sides. A statement then replaces the requests altogether: praise cannot be enumerated. The closing line points away from the speaker's words and toward His own.

When & how to recite

'Aishah reported these words; the chapter places them in the Qunut of the Witr prayer.

Reflection

The three refuges shorten as they proceed, from a pair of named attributes down to a single repeated word. What is fled and what is fled to never change.
