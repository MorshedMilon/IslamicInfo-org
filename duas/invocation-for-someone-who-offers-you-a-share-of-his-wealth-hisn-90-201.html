Dua for someone who gives you money

Hisn al-Muslim 90

((بَارَكَ اللَّهُ لَكَ فِي أَهْلِكَ وَمَالِكَ)).

Baarakallaahu laka fee ahlika wamaalik

(May Allah bless for you, your family and wealth.)

Hisn al-Muslim 90 · Hisn al-Muslim

Source & authenticity

Source: Hisn al-Muslim · Reference: Hisn al-Muslim 90

Translation: Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim), Sa'id al-Qahtani — see site-wide attribution

IslamicInfo presents the source information available in its current verified database and does not issue religious rulings.

Context & meaning

One sentence, naming three things to be blessed: the person addressed, that person's family, and their wealth. The order widens outward from the individual. Allah is spoken about rather than addressed, so the words are directed at the giver rather than upward. Nothing is asked for the speaker, and the wealth just shared is folded into what is blessed.

When & how to recite

Nasa'i records these words, said to someone who offers a share of his wealth.

Reflection

The wealth that prompted the supplication is the last of the three things named. What comes before it — the person, then the family — is not what was offered.
