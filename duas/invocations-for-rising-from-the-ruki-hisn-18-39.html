Dua after rising from ruku (Sahih al-Bukhari 782)

Hisn al-Muslim 18

((رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ، حَمْداً كَثيراً طَيِّباً مُبارَكاً فِيهِ)).

(Rabbana walakal-hamdu hamdan katheeran tayyiban mubarakan feeh.)

(Our Lord, for You is all praise, an abundant beautiful blessed praise.)

Hisn al-Muslim 18 · Hisn al-Muslim

Source & authenticity

Source: Hisn al-Muslim · Reference: Hisn al-Muslim 18

Context & meaning

It addresses Him as our Lord and then makes one statement: all praise is for You. The remainder does not extend that statement but describes the praise itself, stacking three adjectives onto it — abundant, beautiful, blessed. No request follows at any point. The opening word is plural, and nothing later narrows the praise back to the single person saying it.

When & how to recite

Bukhari records this from Rifa'a bin Rafi' Az-Zuraqi, said on rising from the bowing.

Reflection

Three adjectives qualify the praise and none qualifies Him. The supplication spends its length describing what is offered rather than the One receiving it.
