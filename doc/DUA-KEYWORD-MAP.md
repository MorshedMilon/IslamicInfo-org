## Method

- **Every figure in the Volume, headTerm and serpType columns was supplied by the project
  owner** from their own keyword research. None was generated, estimated, or recalled here.
- **Volumes are global unless a cell marks otherwise.** The only marked exceptions are
  `morning adhkar` (4,400) and `morning azkar` (2,200), which the owner recorded as **US-only**
  and therefore **not comparable** to the global 40,500 in the same row.
- **`searchableLabel` is filled only where a volume was supplied**, per instruction, and is
  derived from that row's `headTerm` rather than invented independently.
- **An empty cell means unresearched, not zero and not "no demand."** No cell was filled by
  inference from a similar chapter, by pattern-matching another row, or from model knowledge.
  A guessed figure here would be indistinguishable from a measured one later, which is why
  the 11 unresearched chapters are blank rather than approximate.
- **Chapter names are reproduced exactly as the Hisn al-Muslim corpus records them**, including
  the lower-case opening of row 8, the trailing full stop of row 13, and the apostrophes in
  rows 7 and 10. None was normalised, corrected, or retitled.
- Slugged and shippable counts are measured from `slugs.lock.json` and the corpus.
  "Shippable today" means the record has a transliteration; the 4 gaps are the enlarged
  transliteration debt recorded in ADR-065.

### One naming discrepancy

The brief named this chapter *"What to say after completing prayer"*. The corpus records it as
**"What to say after completing the prayer"** (with *the*). The table uses the corpus wording,
per the "exactly as Hisn has it" rule. Flagging it because the two differ by one word and the
slug follows the corpus, not the brief.

### Risks recorded, not acted on

1. **Row 1 headTerm covers half its chapter.** `morning dua` is a morning query; the chapter is
   *morning and evening* and contains both. Either the chapter needs a label that spans both, or
   the evening half needs its own term. Not resolved here — it is a research question, not a
   naming one.
2. **Row 4 cannibalisation.** `dua after salah` and `dua after prayer` are both 22,200 with
   near-identical SERPs. Treated as one intent. Recorded so that Step 4 does not produce two
   competing pages.
3. **Rows 7 and 10 are adjacent** (bowing, and rising from bowing). Worth checking whether they
   share a SERP before either is given a distinct label.
4. **Rows 3 and 13 have slugs truncated at 60 characters**, dropping a meaningful final word
   ("prayer", "ruler"). Slugs are frozen in `slugs.lock.json` and are **not** changed here.
