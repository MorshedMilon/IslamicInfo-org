Dua for ruku (Riyad as-Salihin 747 · 17:35)

Hisn al-Muslim 17

((سُبُّوُحٌ، قُدُّوسٌ، رَبُّ المَلاَئِكَةِ وَالرُّوحِ)).

Subboohun, Quddoosun, rabbul malaa'ikati war rooh

(Perfect and Holy (He is), Lord of the angles and the Rooh (i.e. Jibra-eel).)

Context & meaning

Two descriptions open it, Perfect and Holy, and He is spoken about rather than addressed. What follows names what He is Lord of, and the choice is particular rather than sweeping: not Lord of the worlds, but Lord of the angels, and then of the Rooh, which the text identifies as Jibra-eel. A whole class is named, then one member of it singled out. Nothing is requested at any point.

When & how to recite

The chapter assigns this to the bowing, on 'Aishah's report.

Reflection

The scope narrows steadily — two absolute descriptions, then a class of creatures, then a single name. It closes at the most particular point rather than the widest.
