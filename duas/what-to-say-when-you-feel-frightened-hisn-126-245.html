Dua when frightened

Hisn al-Muslim 126

((لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ!)).

Laa ilaaha illallaah

(None has the right to be worshipped except Allah.)

Hisn al-Muslim 126 · Hisn al-Muslim

Source & authenticity

Context & meaning

It is a declaration rather than a request, and nothing in it refers to fear or to whatever is feared. The sentence is built as an exclusion: it denies the right of worship to everything, then admits a single exception. No protection is asked for, no danger is named, and the one saying it does not appear anywhere in the wording.

When & how to recite

Abu Sa’id Al-Khudri reported these words, said at the moment fear is felt.

Reflection

The chapter assigns it to fright, yet the words never mention it. What stands in place of a request is a statement about who alone is owed worship.
