Dua for ruku (Sunan an-Nasai 1051 · 17:37)

Hisn al-Muslim 17

((سُبْحَانَ ذِي الْجَبَرُوتِ، وَالْمَلَكُوتِ، وَالْكِبْرِيَاءِ، وَالْعَظَمَةِ)).

Subhaana dheel jabarooti, wal malakooti, wal kibriyaa'i, wal 'azamah

(How perfect He is, The Possessor of total power, sovereignty, magnificence and grandeur.)

Context & meaning

One declaration of perfection, followed by a single title that carries four nouns: power, sovereignty, magnificence and grandeur. The title is possessive — He is named as the One who possesses these, not as one who displays them. The four are not ordered toward a climax, and none is marked as greater than the others. He is referred to throughout in the third person, and nothing is asked for.

When & how to recite

Nasa'i records this from 'Asim bin Humaid, said in the bowing.

Reflection

Two of the four nouns name authority and two name stature. The wording keeps them inside one title rather than separating what He commands from what He is.
