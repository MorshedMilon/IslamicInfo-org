Dua when you find something becoming difficult for you

Hisn al-Muslim 43

((اللَّهُمَّ لاَ سَهْلَ إِلاَّ مَا جَعَلْتَهُ سَهْلاً، وَأَنْتَ تَجْعَلُ الْحَزْنَ إِذَا شِئْتَ سَهْلاً)).

allahumma la sahla illa ma ja'altahu sahla, wa anta taj'alul-hazna idha shi'ta sahla

O Allah, there is no ease except in that which You make easy, and You make the difficult easy if You wish.

Hisn al-Muslim 43 · Hisn al-Muslim

Source & authenticity

Source: Hisn al-Muslim · Reference: Hisn al-Muslim 43

Translation: dua-dhikr (fitrahive), free redistribution

Context & meaning

Both halves say the same thing from opposite ends. The first is a denial: there is no ease anywhere except in what You make easy. The second is an assertion: You make the difficult easy. A condition then closes it — if You wish — which leaves the second statement dependent rather than settled. No request is actually made; the supplication states what is possible and where it comes from.

When & how to recite

The chapter places these words when something becomes difficult.

Reflection

It is said in difficulty and never asks for the difficulty to lift. The closing condition keeps a statement about what He can do from becoming a claim about what He will.
