“In the name of Allah, with whose…” (Hisn al-Muslim 27:86)

Hisn al-Muslim 27

((بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لاَ يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلاَ فِي السّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ)) (ثلاثَ مرَّاتٍ).

bismillahilladhi la yadurru ma'asmihi shay'un fil-ardi wa la fis-sama' wa huwas-sami'ul-'alim

In the name of Allah, with whose name nothing in the earth or the heavens can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.

Hisn al-Muslim 27 · Hisn al-Muslim

Source & authenticity

Source: Hisn al-Muslim · Reference: Hisn al-Muslim 27

Translation: dua-dhikr (fitrahive), free redistribution

Context & meaning

The whole supplication is a single sentence, and the protection it describes is attached to the name it opens with. Nothing in the earth or in the heavens can cause harm alongside that name — the scope is stated in both directions at once rather than limited to any one danger or any one place. It closes by naming two attributes, the All-Hearing and the All-Knowing.

When & how to recite

Said each morning and each evening. The Arabic text marks it to be said three times.

Reflection

Both closing attributes concern attention rather than force. What is offered to the one reciting is that he is heard and known, and no specific threat is ever described or named.
