Dua after wudu (Sunan Abi Dawud 525)

Hisn al-Muslim 9

(أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لاَ شَرِيكَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّداً عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ..).

Ashhadu an laa ilaaha illallaahu wahdahu laa shareeka lah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu warasooluh

(I bear witness that none has the right to be worshipped except Allah, alone without partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His slave and Messenger.)

Context & meaning

Both halves open the same way, with I bear witness, and that repetition is what holds the supplication together. The first testimony concerns Allah: that none has the right to be worshipped except Him, alone and without partner. The second concerns Muhammad ﷺ and gives him two descriptions in a fixed order, His slave before His Messenger. Nothing is requested in either half — the whole of it is testimony.

When & how to recite

The chapter places these words at the completion of the ablution.

Reflection

The order within the second testimony is not incidental. Servanthood is stated first and messengership second, and the wording does not reverse them or state one without the other.
