**Status:** open backlog · 2026-07-31 · read-only record. No page, record or exclusion is changed by this file.

## The rule

**A label that names no source renders as no attribution.** The card omits the attribution element entirely rather than displaying a placeholder that identifies nothing.

**Missing or vacuous attribution NEVER causes exclusion on its own.** An unnamed source is a transparency gap, not a defective record. Where a record below is marked excluded, it is excluded for an unrelated reason — it is a guidance narration, it has no translation, or it is a variant on hold — never for lacking a label.

Resolving an entry means identifying the collection the supplication actually comes from and setting a real `sourceLabel`/`sourceKey`. Never guess one; until then the honest state is silence. Per ADR-058 the corpus has no working generator, so these must be edited in place.

## C. Open question above this backlog

The 187 records labelled `Hisn al-Muslim` (`sourceKey: hisn`) are **not** individually asserted attributions — see the verification recorded in ADR-060. 106 of them carry no citation of any kind, and 183 of the 205 records attributed to "Hisn compilation English" carry the upstream dataset text verbatim, which `meta` states was replaced. That is a larger provenance question than this backlog and is tracked separately.
