Dua when tragedy strikes

Hisn al-Muslim 53

((إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ، اللَّهُمَّ أْجُرْنِي فِي مُصِيبَتِي، وَأَخْلِفْ لِي خَيْرَاً مِنْهَا)).

inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un. allahumma'jurni fi musibati wa akhlif li khayran minha

Indeed we belong to Allah, and indeed to Him we will return. O Allah, reward me for my affliction and replace it with something better.

Hisn al-Muslim 53 · Hisn al-Muslim

Source & authenticity

Source: Hisn al-Muslim · Reference: Hisn al-Muslim 53

Translation: dua-dhikr (fitrahive), free redistribution

Context & meaning

Two facts are stated before anything is asked, and both concern belonging: that we belong to Allah, and that to Him we return. Only afterwards do the requests come, and there are two. The first asks for reward in the affliction itself, treating what has happened as something that earns. The second asks for replacement, and the replacement is qualified — not merely something else, but something better.

When & how to recite

The chapter places these words at the moment tragedy strikes.

Reflection

Nothing here asks for the affliction to be undone. One request finds worth in it as it stands, and the other looks past it to whatever comes next.
