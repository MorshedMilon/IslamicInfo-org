Duas for Provision & Wellbeing

Supplications grouped under provision & wellbeing. Each one is shown with its Arabic text, its meaning in English, and the source it comes from.

2 supplications · every one with its source

((بَارَكَ اللَّهُ لَكَ فِي أَهْلِكَ وَمَالِكَ)).

(Barakal-lahu laka fee ahlika wamalik.)

(May Allah bless for you, your family and wealth.)

Sunan an-Nasai 4693 (it was narrated from Isla'il bin Ibrahim bin 'Abdullah bin Abi Rabi'ah, from his father, that his grandfather said:) · Invocation for someone who offers you a share of his wealth

((بارَكَ اللَّهُ لَكَ فِي أَهْلِكَ وَمَالِكَ، إِنَّمَا جَزَاءُ السَّلَفِ الْحَمْدُ وَالأَدَاءُ)).

(Barakal-lahu laka fee ahlika wamalik, innama jaza-os-salafil-hamdu wal-ada'.)

(May Allah bless for you, your family and wealth. Surely commendation and payment are the reward for a loan.)

Sunan an-Nasai 4693 (it was narrated from Isla'il bin Ibrahim bin 'Abdullah bin Abi Rabi'ah, from his father, that his grandfather said:) · Invocation (upon receipt of the loan) for someone who lends you money
