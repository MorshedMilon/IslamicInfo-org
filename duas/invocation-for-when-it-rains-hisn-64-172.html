Dua when it rains

Hisn al-Muslim 64

((اللَّهُمَّ صَيِّباً نَافِعاً)).

Allaahumma sayyiban naafi'an

O Allah, make it a beneficial rain.

Context & meaning

One request, and it does not ask for rain. The rain has already come; what is asked is that it be of a particular kind. The single qualifier, beneficial, carries the whole supplication, and nothing else is named — no crops, no land, no people. Allah is addressed once at the opening, and the sentence ends immediately after the request.

When & how to recite

The chapter places these words at the falling of rain.

Reflection

What it asks for is a distinction rather than a thing. Rain that has already arrived can still do harm, and the request separates the two possibilities.
