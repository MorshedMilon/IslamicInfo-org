What to say when insulted while fasting

Hisn al-Muslim 75

((إِنِّي صَائِمٌ، إِنِّي صَائِمٌ)).

(Innee sa-im, innee sa-im.)

(I am fasting, I am fasting.)

Hisn al-Muslim 75 · Hisn al-Muslim

Context & meaning

The words are not addressed to Allah at all. They are spoken to the person who has been rude, and they state a fact rather than making a request or returning an insult. The statement is then repeated once, and the repetition adds no new information — the same three words twice over. Nothing is asked for, nothing is denied, and the other person’s conduct is never described or answered directly.

When & how to recite

Recorded in the Muwatta of Malik, said while fasting when someone is rude.

Reflection

It answers rudeness by naming the speaker’s own state rather than the other’s behaviour. Repetition is the only emphasis available, since no further words are ever added.
