“O Allah, Knower of the unseen and…” (Hisn al-Muslim 27:85)

Hisn al-Muslim 27

((اللَّهُمَّ عَالِمَ الغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ فَاطِرَ السَّمَوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، رَبَّ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ وَمَلِيكَهُ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ نَفْسِي، وَمِنْ شَرِّ الشَّيْطانِ وَشَرَكِهِ، وَأَنْ أَقْتَرِفَ عَلَى نَفْسِي سُوءاً، أَوْ أَجُرَّهُ إِلَى مُسْلِمٍ)).

Allaahumma 'aalimal ghaybi wash shahaadati, faatiras samaawaati wal ardi, rabba kulli shay'in wamaleekahu, ashhadu an laa ilaaha illaa anta, a'oodhu bika min sharri nafsee, wamin sharrish shaytaani washarakihi, wa an aqtarifa 'alaa nafsee soo'an, aw ajurrahu ilaa muslim

O Allah, Knower of the unseen and the seen, Creator of the heavens and the earth, Lord of all things and their Sovereign. I bear witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except You. I seek refuge in You from the evil of myself, Satan and his soldiers (temptations to commit shirk against Allah), and I (seek refuge in You) from committing evil against myself or dragging it to a Muslim.

Context & meaning

Three titles open the supplication before any request is made: Knower of the unseen and the seen, Creator of the heavens and the earth, and Lord of all things and their Sovereign. A statement of witness follows, that there is no deity worthy of worship except Him. Only then does the asking begin, and the first evil named is not an external one — refuge is sought from the evil of my own self, before Satan and his soldiers are mentioned at all.

When & how to recite

Placed by the chapter at morning and at evening.

Reflection

The closing clause reaches past the one praying. Having asked protection from committing evil against himself, the wording asks the same regarding evil dragged toward another Muslim.
