Dua for debt relief (Sunan an-Nasai 5462 · 41:137)

Hisn al-Muslim 41

((اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْبُخْلِ وَالْجُبْنِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ)).

(Allahumma innee aAAoothu bika minal-hammi walhuzn, walAAajzi walkasal, walbukhl, waljubn, wadalAAid-dayni waghalabatir-rijal)

(O Allah, I take refuge in You from anxiety and sorrow, weakness and laziness, miserliness and cowardice, the burden of debts and from being over powered by men.)

Context & meaning

Only one of the eight things named concerns debt, and it sits seventh in the list. The rest are states of mind and character — anxiety and sorrow, weakness and laziness, miserliness and cowardice — followed at the end by being overpowered by men. Refuge is sought once, at the opening, and governs everything after it. The wording never singles debt out or treats it differently from the seven around it.

When & how to recite

Anas bin Malik reported these words, gathered by the chapter under the settling of a debt.

Reflection

The chapter files it under debt, yet the wording gives debt a single clause among eight and never returns to it. What surrounds it is interior rather than financial.
