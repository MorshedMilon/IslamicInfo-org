Dua for breaking fast (Hisn al-Muslim 68:176)

Hisn al-Muslim 68

((ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ العُرُوقُ، وَثَبَتَ الْأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ)).

dhahabadh-dhama'u wabtallatil-'uruqu wa thabatal-ajru insha allah

The thirst has gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is confirmed, Insha Allah.

Hisn al-Muslim 68 · Hisn al-Muslim

Source & authenticity

Source: Hisn al-Muslim · Reference: Hisn al-Muslim 68

Translation: dua-dhikr (fitrahive), free redistribution

IslamicInfo presents the source information available in its current verified database and does not issue religious rulings.

Context & meaning

Three statements in a row, and none of them is a request. The first two report physical facts about the body: the thirst has gone, the veins are moistened. The third moves to something that cannot be observed — the reward is confirmed — and qualifies it immediately with Insha Allah. That qualifier attaches only to the third clause, leaving the two bodily statements unconditioned.

When & how to recite

The chapter places these words at the breaking of the fast.

Reflection

Certainty drops as the sentence proceeds. What the body can verify is stated plainly, and what it cannot is stated with a condition attached to it.
