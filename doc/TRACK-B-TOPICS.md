**Why these are flagged**

- `Qur'an 2:200` — Already flagged `contextual` in the corpus: the Qur'an quotes this as the request of those who ask only for this world. 2:201 is the praised form.
- `Qur'an 2:260` — Ibrahim asking to be shown how the dead are revived — a request within a narrative, not a supplication taught for recitation.
- `Qur'an 3:9` — A statement of belief about the Day of Gathering, not a petition.
- `Qur'an 3:36` — "My Lord, I have delivered a female" — a statement of fact.
- `Qur'an 3:40` — Zakariyya's question about how he will have a son.
- `Qur'an 3:47` — Maryam's question about how she will have a child.
- `Qur'an 4:77` — The reluctant asking why fighting was decreed — a complaint the verse reports, not a dua.
- `Qur'an 6:128` — Speech of jinn and men on the Day of Judgement.
- `Qur'an 7:38` — The damned in the Fire asking for their misleaders to be doubly punished.
- `Qur'an 7:143` — Musa asking to see his Lord — a request within a narrative, answered with a rebuke.
- `Qur'an 7:155` — Musa questioning whether his people would be destroyed for the acts of the foolish.
- `Qur'an 10:88` — Musa's imprecation against Pharaoh, asking that his wealth be obliterated. A prophet's dua, but not one taught for general recitation — needs an editorial call.
- `Qur'an 11:45` — Nuh appealing for his son — the very next verse rebukes the appeal.
- `Qur'an 14:36` — Ibrahim stating that the idols have misled many.
- `Qur'an 14:38` — A statement that Allah knows what is concealed and declared.
- `Qur'an 14:44` — The wrongdoers on the Day of Judgement asking to be sent back.
- `Qur'an 15:36` — **Speech of Iblis**, asking to be reprieved until the Day of Resurrection.
- `Qur'an 15:39` — **Speech of Iblis**, vowing to make disobedience attractive to mankind.

- `Qur'an 33:67` — The damned blaming their leaders.
- `Qur'an 33:68` — The damned asking that their leaders be doubly punished.
