1. Educational Purpose — Not Religious Rulings

IslamicInfo provides educational reference material: Qur'anic text and translations, hadith with their gradings, supplications, and learning tools. IslamicInfo does not issue fatwas or religious rulings. Content is presented with citations to its sources so you can verify everything independently. For rulings on your personal circumstances, consult a qualified scholar.

2. Calculators and Tools

Tools such as the Zakat calculator and the Inheritance (Farā'iḍ) calculator are provided for educational illustration. They compute from the Qur'anic texts and authenticated hadith cited on their pages, and they cannot account for every personal, legal, or jurisdictional circumstance.

Results from calculators are not legal, financial, or religious advice. For the distribution of an actual estate or the discharge of actual Zakat obligations, consult a qualified scholar and, where relevant, a licensed professional in your jurisdiction.
