Dua for anguish (Sahih al-Bukhari 6109)

Hisn al-Muslim 35

((لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ الْعَظِيمُ الْحَلِيمُ، لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ، لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ رَبُّ السَّمَوَاتِ وَرَبُّ الْأَرْضِ وَرَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْكَرِيمِ)).

(La ilaha illal-lahul-AAatheemul-haleem, la ilaha illal-lahu rabbul-AAarshil-AAatheem, la ilaha illal-lahu rabbus-samawati warabbul-ardi warabbul-AAarshil-kareem.)

(None has the right to be worshipped except Allah Forbearing. None has the right to be worshipped except Allah, Lord of the magnificent throne. None has the right to be worshipped except Allah, Lord of the heavens, Lord of the Earth and Lord of the noble throne.)

Context & meaning

One sentence is repeated three times, and only its ending changes. Each repetition declares that none has the right to be worshipped except Allah, and each then names Him differently. The first names Him Forbearing. The second names Him Lord of the magnificent throne. The third widens furthest — Lord of the heavens, Lord of the Earth, and Lord of the noble throne — naming three domains where the earlier statements named one.

When & how to recite

The chapter places these words at a time of anguish.

Reflection

For words said in distress, nothing at all is requested. The supplication repeats a single declaration and spends its length enlarging the description of the One it is addressed to.

What do these words mean?

The English of it is: “None has the right to be worshipped except Allah Forbearing. None has the right to be worshipped except Allah, Lord of the magnificent throne. None has the right to be worshipped except Allah, Lord of the heavens, Lord of the Earth and Lord of the noble throne.” One sentence is repeated three times, and only its ending changes.
